About Kula Maldives

From our workshop

We started in Malé in 2020. We’re a locally owned and operated printing, signage, and fabrication business. From our workshop in Malé, we handle everything from everyday document copying and large-format canvas prints to custom LED signs, vehicle wraps, and laser-cut acrylic. For signage projects, we take care of the design, fabrication, mounting brackets, and installation.

Our mission

Our mission is to exceed expectations on every job through careful workmanship, straightforward service, and fair pricing. We help you choose the materials and finishes that suit your project and budget, communicate clearly throughout the job, and work to deliver on time.

Print standards

We aim to meet international quality standards in our print work. On a finished job, that means sharp detail, consistent colour, and a finish that suits where and how it will be used.
