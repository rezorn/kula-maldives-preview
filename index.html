What We Do

From Artwork to Installation

Have something in mind? Stop by Kula and tell us about it. We’ll show you how it could look and make changes with you. Once the design’s set, we’ll take the measurements, make it, and fit it on-site.

  1. 01 · Share Your Idea

    Drop by the workshop or send us your idea, a sketch, or a reference photo.

  2. 02 · See It in a Mockup

    We turn your idea into a visual mockup so you can see how it could look.

  3. 03 · Fine-Tune the Design

    Tell us what you’d like to change. We’ll refine the design with you.

  1. 04 · Measure on Site

    We visit the site, take measurements, and check how everything will fit.

  2. 05 · Approve & Produce

    We confirm the final artwork, sizes, materials, and finish with you, then get to work.

  3. 06 · Install on Site

    We fit the finished work, check the details, and hand it over.
