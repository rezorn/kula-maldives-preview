Laser Cutting & Engraving

We create custom-cut and engraved pieces for signage, displays, events, and branded products. Working from your logo, artwork, or a rough sketch, we can make anything from a single custom cutout to a batch of matching pieces.

Custom Cutting

We cut lettering, logos, shapes, and individual components to suit your design. Whether you need raised lettering for a shop sign, a freestanding display for an event, or small pieces for a branded product, we can help you choose the right material, thickness, and finish.

  • Signage and lettering including dimensional letters, logos, plaques, and door signs.
  • Custom cutouts for event backdrops, display stands, props, and decorative panels.
  • Branded products such as keychains, tags, and display pieces.

Laser Engraving

We engrave names, logos, lettering, and patterns onto wood and other suitable materials. Engraving works well for personalised gifts, nameplates, branded plaques, and decorative details. We can adjust the artwork for the size and material so fine details and small lettering stay clear.

Materials & Artwork

Wood is one of our main materials for laser cutting and engraving. We also work with acrylic for projects such as raised lettering and keychains. For other sheet materials, we’ll confirm the exact material and the cutting method that suits it. If you already have a vector file, send it over. We can also help prepare your artwork from a sketch or reference image.
