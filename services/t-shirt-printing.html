T-Shirt Printing

We print custom T-shirts for businesses, teams, events, and personal projects. We use direct-to-film (DTF) printing for designs up to A3, from small chest logos to larger graphics on the front or back. Send us your artwork and we’ll help you choose a print size and placement that look right on the shirt.

We also take bulk orders for corporate T-shirts, staff uniforms, and event shirts. Let us know your preferred shirt colour and how many you need in each size, and we’ll put together a quote. Please allow 4–7 days for delivery on bulk orders.
