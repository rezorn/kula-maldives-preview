Custom LED Signage

We design, fabricate, and install custom LED signs for shops, cafés, offices, and commercial spaces. From a slim edge-lit sign above a café entrance to illuminated channel letters across a storefront, we build each sign around your brand, the installation site, and where people will see it.

Types of LED Signs We Make

  • Slim edge-lit signboards suit entrances, shopfronts, and interior walls where a slim profile matters. The LEDs sit along the edge of an acrylic panel, lighting up the design while keeping the sign compact.
  • Illuminated channel letters and logos give your name or logo a three-dimensional finish. Depending on the design, we can light the front faces for clear visibility or create a halo of light behind the letters.
  • LED lightboxes illuminate a printed face from behind, making them a good fit for logos, menus, and graphics that need to stay visible after dark.
  • Projecting signs extend out from the wall so people can spot your business as they approach along the street. We design the bracket to suit the sign and its mounting location.
  • Illuminated totem signs are freestanding signs for entrances, commercial properties, and wayfinding. They work well when the sign needs to be visible away from the building façade.

From Design to Installation

  1. Site planning and design. We review the location, available space, and viewing distance. Then we work out the size, layout, materials, and lighting style. A sign meant to be read from across the street calls for different lettering and placement than a sign above a reception desk.
  2. Fabrication and mounting. We build the sign and make the mounting brackets, backing panels, or support frames the project needs. We plan the mounting around the site, including how far the sign should sit from the wall and where the wiring can run.
  3. On-site installation. We fit the sign and its supports, align the lettering or panels, and check the lighting once the sign is in place.
