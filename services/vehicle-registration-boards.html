Vehicle Number Plates & Vessel Registration Markings

We make standard registration number plates for cars and motorcycles. Each plate is laid out to suit the vehicle, with clear lettering and consistent spacing so the registration number is easy to read.

For speedboats and ferries, we offer custom-cut registration stencils and ready-to-apply stickers. Our stencils guide the placement of each letter and number when painting directly onto the hull, helping you keep the markings aligned on both sides of the vessel. If you prefer an adhesive finish, our registration stickers come with the lettering already arranged for application.
