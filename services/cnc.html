CNC Cutting

We provide custom CNC cutting for PVC foam board, metal, and wood. We cut lettering, logos, characters, and custom shapes for signage, trophies, retail displays, and events. Whether you need a single piece or a matching batch, we can work from your artwork, dimensions, or a reference image.

Materials We Work With

  • PVC foam board works well for lightweight lettering, character cutouts, event graphics, and display panels. It’s a practical choice for temporary setups and pieces you need to move between venues.
  • Metal suits projects that need a rigid piece or a metallic finish, such as cut lettering, logos, plaques, and decorative panels. We’ll confirm the available metal, thickness, and finish based on your design.
  • Wood brings a warm, natural finish to signs, trophy bases, decorative cutouts, and display pieces. We can help you choose a suitable board and thickness based on the size of the piece and how it will be used.

What We Make

  • Signs, logos, and lettering for shopfronts, reception walls, and branded displays. Use individual cut letters for a raised effect or mount them on a backing panel.
  • Custom trophies and awards for competitions, corporate events, and celebrations. Build the design around your event logo or a custom silhouette, with space for names, titles, and dates.
  • Character and prop cutouts for photo areas, birthdays, promotions, and stage displays. We can plan the piece around a freestanding base or a backdrop mount.
  • Retail and event displays including product stands, branded panels, and custom shapes for stalls and exhibitions.
  • Decorative panels and one-off pieces for interiors, feature walls, and projects that need a specific shape or fit.

From Artwork to Finished Piece

Send us your design, sketch, or reference photo. We’ll review the scale, material thickness, and how the piece will stand or mount. For designs with fine details, we can adjust the artwork so narrow sections and small lettering cut cleanly. If your project also needs printed graphics, a backing panel, or installation, let us know when you request a quote so we can plan the finished piece together.
